The development

Riverside living, a short ride from the city

Grand Dunman is a 99-year leasehold condominium on Dunman Road in District 15, developed by Grand Dunman Pte Ltd and brought to you by SingHaiyi Group and CSC Land Group. Its 1,008 homes are spread across six luxury blocks and one Grand block, set on a site of about 271,600 sq ft beside the Geylang River.

The development is complete, with over 40 facilities including the Jewel Clubhouse, a grand pool, a tennis court and garden zones. The Geylang Park Connector runs past the gate, leading towards the Sports Hub, Marina Bay and East Coast Park.

Q&A

Common questions

Who is the developer?

Grand Dunman Pte Ltd (Developer Licence C1456). The project is brought to you by SingHaiyi Group and CSC Land Group.

Is Grand Dunman completed?

Yes. TOP has been obtained, so viewings are of actual units. The showflat has closed. Book a viewing.

What is the tenure?

99 years leasehold, commencing 12 September 2022.

How many units are there?

1,008 residential units in seven blocks, plus five shops. Availability changes quickly, so ask for the latest list.

How far is the MRT?

Dakota MRT on the Circle Line is about a 2-minute walk. Paya Lebar (Circle and East-West Lines) is one stop away, and Stadium two.

Which schools are nearby?

Within about 1 km: EtonHouse International School Broadrick, Tanjong Katong Girls' School, Chung Cheng High School (Main), Kong Hwa School, Tanjong Katong Secondary School, Canadian International School (Tanjong Katong) and Tanjong Katong Primary School. For Primary 1 registration, check the exact home-school distance for your block on OneMap, as MOE and SLA determine eligibility.

What does the "-H" in a unit type mean?

Types ending in "-H", such as 1BR S1-H or 5BR G1-H, are top-floor units with a high-ceiling strata void over part of the living area. The void is included in the stated area.

Are the show unit furnishings included?

No. Furniture, decor and loose fittings shown in show units and photos are for display only. The fittings supplied are set out in the Sale and Purchase Agreement.

Can I buy a shop unit?

Yes, subject to availability. Commercial units are bought on different terms from homes: ABSD does not apply, but GST is generally payable and CPF cannot be used. See the shop units.

Can foreigners buy here?

Yes. Condominium units are not restricted for foreign buyers, though ABSD applies at the prevailing rates for your profile. Speak to us and check IRAS for the latest rules.

Is this the developer's official website?

No. This site is run by AMP Global Innovations LLC to share project information and arrange viewings through Cherine Koh of Huttons Asia Pte Ltd. Read the disclaimer.
